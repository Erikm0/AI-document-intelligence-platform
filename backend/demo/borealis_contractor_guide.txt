Borealis Labs Contractor Guide
==============================

Working Arrangements

Contractors at Borealis Labs may work remotely five days per week, but they must attend the quarterly planning week on site in Tromso.

Contractor meal expenses are reimbursed up to 30 euros per day when travelling on behalf of Borealis Labs.

Security for Contractors

Contractors must report security incidents to the Borealis service desk within 24 hours. Personal devices may not be used for Borealis work.
Invoicing and Termination

Contractors invoice Borealis Labs monthly and invoices are paid within 45 days. Borealis Labs does not pay late payment interest to contractors.

Either Borealis Labs or the contractor may end an engagement with 14 days written notice.

Laboratory Equipment

The cryogenic test chamber in Building 4 operates at 77 kelvin and must be inspected by a certified engineer every six months.
